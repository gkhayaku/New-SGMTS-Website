The project

Announced in the 2023 Policy Address and taken forward in 2024, the SGMTS will run mostly on dedicated roads, rise over busy junctions, and adopt a green road-based mode that needs no physical rail tracks, so routes and frequencies can flex as the new towns grow.

01

Green & flexible design

The new town is planned around a Green Transit Corridor: the mass transit system, footpaths and cycle tracks together, promoting low-carbon commuting. With no physical tracks or overhead cables, the road-based mode can flexibly adjust routing and service frequencies, minimise environmental impact, and save construction time and costs.

  • No rail tracks
  • No overhead cables
  • Flexible routing
  • Innovative paving under study

02

Dedicated roads, grade separated

Most of the system runs on dedicated roads; at major junctions, grade separation such as elevated sections keeps the SGMTS clear of other traffic — fewer shared junctions by design.

  • Dedicated roads
  • Grade-separated junctions
  • Elevated sections

03

Smart traffic, early warning

A Real-time Adaptive Traffic Signal System dynamically allocates signal time for smoother flow, and the future operator will be encouraged to use V2X technology for early warning of potential risks.

  • Adaptive signals
  • V2X early warning
  • Smoother flow

04

Passenger-friendly by design

Low-floor vehicles with multiple doors, off-board fare collection at stations, and covered waiting areas make boarding faster and waiting pleasant, designed around the everyday trip, not the other way round.

  • Low-floor boarding
  • Multiple doors
  • Off-board fares
  • Covered waiting areas
